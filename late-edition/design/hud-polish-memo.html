HUD Polish

What's broken · what to ship · and why the player still has no idea what to do

The chrome around the play area is doing too much and showing too little. Three rows of header eat 200px before the world starts; the bottom dock has no floor under it, so the only panels that explain what the player is looking at are clipped to a single line of ghost-text. We're going to fix that today.

04 First 60 seconds

Why the player doesn't know how to play — and a five-beat script that fixes it

The current build asks the player to know what a "reporter," a "whisper," "press HP," and "The Docks" are, then dispatch one into another, all without showing them which thing on screen is which. Onboarding is a five-beat thing, not a one-line italic. The Old Man should talk, point, wait, confirm, then get out of the way.

0:00 — Beat 1

Meet your hands

Camera floats over the newsroom. Greta is already selected. The dock shows her.

"That's you, kid. Well — your hands. Greta Halloran. She runs."
0:10 — Beat 2

Meet the board

Camera arcs to the corkboard tile. Whisper card pulses on the dock's middle panel.

"One whisper came in overnight. Vice angle. Unverified — worth nothing 'til it's pinned."
0:22 — Beat 3

Click to select

Target ring snaps onto Greta. The bubble waits for the click. Nothing else is interactive.

"Click her. Yeah, just left-click. The dock'll tell you what she's holding."
0:35 — Beat 4

Right-click to dispatch

Target ring jumps to The Docks tile (which we now show, even if it's offscreen — the camera pans). Path preview draws.

"Right-click the dock. She'll walk. Takes ink to publish what she finds — you got 20."
0:55 — Beat 5

Watch the clock

Old Man bubble fades. Deadline tile pulses amber once. Game is now in player hands.

"Deadline's 11:24. Don't be late. — I'll be in the back."

This is a separate file from the HUD work but it lives or dies on it: if the dock is invisible, beat 2 ("meet the board") doesn't exist; if there's no target ring, beat 3 is a guessing game.

05 What I'd cut · what I'd keep

A short list of the controversial bits, decided

Cut

The standalone Settings button on the canvas

Floating UI on the play area is for things the player needs during play. Settings is not that. Roll it into ?.

Saves 32px of canvas at top-left · removes a click target that competes with selection
Cut

The "v1.4.0" version string in the masthead

Player doesn't care. Bury it in ? → About. Or in a corner of the pause screen.

Looks like a dev build, breaks the in-world feel
Keep

The rotated-diamond minimap

It's good. Distinctive. Reads as a folded press dispatch. Just move it 12px down off the top bar.

High-character chrome is rare; protect it
Keep

The masthead serif typography

Bodoni for the brand mark in the top bar. Garamond italic for the Old Man. Mono for stats. The voice is already there.

The look is the differentiator; the layout is the problem
Cut

The "Note from the Old Man" persistent strip

An always-on banner trains the eye to ignore it. Replace with a bubble that appears, gets dismissed, and comes back when something new is true.

Persistence kills urgency
Keep

Heat and Press HP as first-class stats

They're the most game-y signals in the HUD. Tint them (amber/red) so they read at a glance and feel like a heartbeat.

A glanceable danger signal is half the genre
— The desk
