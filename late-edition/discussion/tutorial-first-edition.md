**Status:** open · needs a paper session before MVP polish
**Cites:** Design Doc §05 · §13

The diegetic onboarding beat.

The frame: "Your old man left notes." The player inherits a Bureau with a
half-finished assignment book. Edition 1 walks the player through the loop
**through diegesis, not through a tooltip overlay**:

1. A lead is already pinned to the corkboard. The note says *"Send a reporter
   to Riverside, kid."*
2. Click reporter → click district → reporter walks.
3. Reporter returns with the lead. Note on the corkboard: *"That's a lead.
   Send it down to the darkroom to verify it."*
4. Darkroom verifies. Note: *"Now plate it. Press goes at 6."*
5. Deadline fires. First edition prints. The Editor's note: *"Welcome to the
   paper."*

This is the spine; the script needs writing. Settle at the post-0.6 re-eval
(decide tutorial shape) and write the copy in the milestone-0.10 PR.
