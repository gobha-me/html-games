# discussion · observer-violence

**Status:** open · pre-architecture · not a commitment
**Cites:** Design Doc §10 (fail-state) · Rival AI Spec (Observer)

Should the Observer ever fight back?

Today's posture: the Observer is a journalistic rival, not a goon-shop — it
exposes and discredits but doesn't raid the Bureau. The press is destroyable
only by the Evening Star.

The design space worth holding open: a **partner-fund Tier IV mechanism**
where, late in a run, the Observer can fund another rival's raid (laundering
violence through someone else's hands). This keeps the Observer's character
intact while giving them a teeth-baring move once the player is consistently
out-truthing them.

This is parked, not committed. Revisit when the Observer's milestone enters
the N-window (likely Tier-1).
