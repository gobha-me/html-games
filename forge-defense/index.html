⚒️ How to Play — Forge Defense

🎯 Goal

Defend your forge from waves of enemies. Build turrets, craft ammo, and survive to Wave 50 for victory!

🏗️ Building

  • Press 1–9 to select a building from the sidebar
  • Click on the map to place it
  • Press R to rotate before placing
  • Press Q or Esc to cancel placement
  • Right-click an existing building to delete it (refunds 50%)
  • Press P to pause / resume the game at any time

⛏️ Resources & Crafting

  • Mines auto-harvest raw ore, coal, and stone
  • Furnaces smelt ore + coal into plates
  • Assemblers turn plates into circuits and ammo
  • Use the Manual Craft buttons for quick one-off crafting

⚔️ Combat

  • Turrets auto-fire at enemies in range
  • Gun turrets need ammo; cannon turrets need shells
  • Keep your supply lines running — turrets stop firing without ammo!

💾 Save / Load

Use the 💾 and 📂 buttons in the sidebar. Your progress is stored locally in the browser.
