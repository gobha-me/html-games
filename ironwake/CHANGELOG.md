# Changelog — IRONWAKE // CONVOY

## [1.0.0] — 2026-10-20

### Added
- Initial release of **IRONWAKE // CONVOY** (`ironwake/`), a 2.5D tactical armored-train & wasteland convoy defense game (closing #15).
- **2.5D Scorched Salt-Flat & Armored Dreadnought Renderer (`js/renderer.js`)**:
  - High-speed parallax scrolling alkali salt-flat terrain, canyon rock mesas, scrolling timber crossties, specular steel rails, elliptical Aegis Void Shield dome, and 5 modular armored railcars with rotating twin-barrel turrets, glowing furnace cowcatcher, floodlight cone, and steam/ember exhaust plumes.
- **Custom Dieselpunk Pixel-Art Sprites & Illustrations (`assets/sprites/`)**:
  - Watermark-free transparent pixel-art vehicle sprites for Sand-Skiff Interceptor (`raider_skiff.png`), Ironclad Harpoon Rig (`harpoon_rig.png`), Valkyrie Assault Gyrocopter (`gyrocopter.png`), and Goliath Canyon Siege Crawler Boss (`siege_boss.png`), plus Commander Vane Kael portrait (`portrait_commander.png`) and Dreadnought Locomotive emblem (`locomotive_emblem.png`).
- **Real-Time Steam Reactor Manifold, Harpoon Tethers & Subsystem Targeting (`js/sim.js`, `js/main.js`)**:
  - 10-bar Steam Reactor power routing across Weapons Overclock, Aegis Void Shield, Locomotive Thrust, and Repair Drones & Vents with 4 one-click tactical presets.
  - Targetable enemy subsystems (`[Z]` Turret Mount, `[X]` Drive Engine, `[C]` Harpoon Winch), high-tension steel harpoon cables, 3 Commander Tactical Orders (`[Q]` Counter-Flak & Sever Harpoons, `[W]` Thermal Steam Vent Purge, `[E]` Overdrive Barrage), and per-car MK upgrades across a 4-stage wasteland route.
